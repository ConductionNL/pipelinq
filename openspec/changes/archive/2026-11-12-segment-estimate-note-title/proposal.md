## Why

icon. The Dq boards title a tonal note in a dialog 14px bold on a 1.5 line (DqArchiveren "Daarna alleen lezen",
DqBesluit "Publiceren doet u daarna"); the Pq board is amended to that in design-system #866 and draws the title 12px
after the icon.
