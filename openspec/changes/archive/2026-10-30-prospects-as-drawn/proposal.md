# Proposal: prospects-as-drawn

## Summary

pl2 PqProspects: the prospects page had no line under its title, the ideal customer profile card spread its facts over a grid with the labels apart from their values and without Edit profile, and the toolbar had no lenses and no Filter. Now:

- the line under the title: how many companies fit, best first, and when the list was refreshed;
- the profile card collapses, carries Edit profile, and lists each fact as a label over its value, keywords included;
- the toolbar: the lenses All, Good fit and New this week with their counts, the Filter menu (place), and a search that narrows the list.
