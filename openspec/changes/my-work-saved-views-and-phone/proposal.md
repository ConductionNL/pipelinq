# My work: saved views and the phone view

## Why

`my-work-roadmap` brought back twelve unbuilt My work requirements. Ten are built and archived (25 Nov 2026, synced into
`openspec/specs/my-work`). Two are not, and each needs more than configuration: saved views must store the My work bar's
type, Show completed, priority, pipeline and scope as OpenRegister saved views, which the dashboard page's filter bar
cannot do yet, and the phone view follows board `PqMobiel`. This change carries those two so the rest could be archived.

## What changes

| ID | Requirement | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| REQ-MW-150 | Saved views on My work | Enterprise |
| REQ-MW-170 | Phone view keeps headers and filters in reach | V1 |

The requirement text is moved unchanged from `my-work-roadmap`.
