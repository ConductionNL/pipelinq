# product-detail Specification

## Purpose
How the product page lays out its figures and tabs.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The product page's figures and tab strip are as tall as their content

The ProductDetail figures and tab strip SHALL take the height of their content, so no card in the open tab is cut off.

#### Scenario: PqProduct Overview

- **THEN** "Gebruikt op deals" shows its bottom edge and "Images & spec sheets" stands under it
- **AND** the tabs start 20px under the figures
