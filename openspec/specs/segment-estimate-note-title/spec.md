# segment-estimate-note-title Specification

## Purpose
How the audience estimate note in the segment dialog is titled.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The segment estimate note titles as a Dq dialog note

The estimate note in the segment builder SHALL title its count 14px bold on a 1.5 line height, 12px after its icon.

#### Scenario: PqSegmentBouwer

- **GIVEN** a segment whose rules are complete
- **THEN** "Geschat aantal leden: 412" is 14px bold on a 21px line, 12px after the people icon
