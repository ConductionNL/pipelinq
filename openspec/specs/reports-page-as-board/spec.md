# reports-page-as-board Specification

## Purpose
The Reports page as the board's catalogue of reports.

## Requirements

### Requirement: The Reports page is the board's catalogue

The Reports page SHALL be a reports catalogue with a "New analysis report" header button to Nextcloud Analytics and the
"Missing a report?" note, and SHALL keep every report card it has, including SLA attainment.
