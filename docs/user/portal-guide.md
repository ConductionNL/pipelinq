# Customer portal — user guide

## Signing in

Go to your supplier's portal (e.g. `https://klant.example.nl` or the link they
sent you) and sign in with your email and password. If two-factor authentication
is required you will be asked to set it up with an authenticator app (Google
Authenticator, Authy, …) and then enter a 6-digit code.

Forgot your password? Use **Wachtwoord vergeten? / Forgotten your password?** —
you will receive a reset link by email (valid 30 minutes).

## Viewing invoices, contracts and orders

The **Documents** tab shows your invoices, contracts and orders. Use **Download**
to open a document; the link is short-lived for your security.

## Submitting and following up requests

Under **Requests** you can:

- See the status of your requests (e.g. *Wachten op uw reactie* / *Awaiting your
  reply*).
- Open a request to read the messages and reply.
- Submit a new request with a subject, message and (optional) attachments
  (max 25 MB per file).

## Updating your details

Under **My details** you can change your name, phone, address, language and (for
business accounts) job title. Changing your email sends a verification link to
the new address — your login stays the same until you confirm it.

## Sharing access (business accounts)

Under **Shared access** you can let a colleague see your invoices/contracts or
submit requests on your behalf, for a chosen period, and revoke it at any time.

## Privacy and your data

Under **Privacy** you can:

- **Request my data** — download a machine-readable copy of everything the portal
  holds about you (AVG/GDPR Art. 15).
- **Close my account** — confirmed by an email link. Closing is permanent.

## Session timeout

For your security your session expires after a period of inactivity. A warning
appears 60 seconds before expiry with **Extend session** and **Log out** buttons.
