# Open Pipelinq for the first time

The first time you open Pipelinq, you land on the **Sales overview** dashboard — revenue, pipeline coverage, and win performance at a glance. An admin can switch the default landing page to **My Work** (your personal queue of leads, requests, and follow-ups) under [Manage Pipelinq settings](../admin/06-admin-settings.md).

## Goal

Recognise the parts of the Pipelinq workspace and know where to find the actions you'll use in every other tutorial.

## Prerequisites

- Pipelinq is installed and enabled by your administrator.
- You have at least read access to the org's clients and pipelines.

## Steps

### 1. Click the Pipelinq icon in the Nextcloud app launcher

Or visit `/apps/pipelinq/` directly. The workspace renders with the **Sales overview** dashboard front and centre, and the grouped navigation down the left.

![Pipelinq first launch](/screenshots/user-guide/user/01-first-launch.png)

### 2. Scan the navigation

The left navigation is organised into collapsible **sections**, each grouping the pages for one part of the business. Click a section header to expand it:

- **Customer Support** — the support workplace, requests, complaints, and contact moments.
- **Sales** — the Sales dashboard, Clients, Contacts, Leads, the Pipeline board, Pipelines, and contracts/forecast.
- **Operational** — the operational dashboard, Tasks, My Work, and Queues.
- **Point of Sale** — register transactions, refunds, cash shifts, Z-reports, and POS staff/roles.
- **Product catalog** — products and catalog management.
- **Marketing** — segmentation, blasts, and campaign analytics.
- **Reports & Compliance** — analytics dashboards and BI export jobs.
- **Messaging (WhatsApp/SMS)** — outbound messaging channels.

Below the sections sit **Documentation**, **Features & roadmap**, and the **Settings** cog. Sections only appear if the matching feature is enabled and you have access, so your menu may show fewer than the full set.

![Pipelinq navigation](/screenshots/user-guide/user/01-navigation.png)

### 3. Open the global search

The unified-search icon in the Nextcloud top bar (magnifying glass) searches across clients, leads, requests, and contact moments. Pipelinq registers each as a search provider so a single query surfaces matches from every record type.

![Global search](/screenshots/user-guide/user/01-search.png)

## Verification

- The **Sales overview** dashboard is visible with its KPI cards and charts (or the **My Work** queue, if your admin set that as the default landing page).
- The left navigation shows the collapsible sections (Sales, Operational, and any others enabled for you).

## Common issues

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Pipelinq isn't in the app launcher | Ask an admin to enable the app at **Settings → Apps**. |
| The dashboard widgets all read *No items found* | Expected on a fresh install. They fill in as you add clients, leads, and requests. |
| Lists load but the *Add Item* dialog has no form fields | The Pipelinq register has not been imported. See [Manage Pipelinq settings](../admin/06-admin-settings.md). |
