# Pipelinq

CRM and pipeline management for Nextcloud. Clients, contacts, leads, and
quotes as typed OpenRegister records. No separate sales database, no second
login.

## What you get

- Drag-and-drop lead pipeline with configurable stages, values, and close
  probabilities.
- Clients and contacts as a typed OpenRegister schema, with a 360° client
  view and citation-stable IDs.
- Quotes that become the contract — one record carries the lines, price,
  and client from quotation through to an active contract.
- Contactmoment logging (calls, emails, visits) and scheduled CSV (or
  Parquet/JSONL) data export for reporting.

## Getting started

Install Pipelinq from the [Nextcloud app store](https://apps.nextcloud.com/apps/pipelinq)
or enable it in your Nextcloud admin settings, then point it at an
OpenRegister register on the settings page.
