# Saved replies and sending again

You type the same answer many times a day. Write it once as a saved reply and pick it wherever you answer a client. When a WhatsApp or SMS message fails, send it again from the message itself.

## Make a saved reply

1. Open **Saved replies** in the menu, under the customer support group.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Give the reply a title, for example *Opening hours*. Agents look for this title in the list.
4. Write the text. You can use these placeholders:
   - `{{client.name}}`: the client's name
   - `{{contact.name}}`: the contact person's name
   - `{{ticket.title}}`: the title of the ticket you answer
   - `{{agent.name}}`: your own name
5. Choose the channels where the reply fits: SMS, WhatsApp, email or portal.
6. Optional: set a language. Clients who asked to be written to in that language see this reply first.

Switch **Active** off to hide a reply without removing it. Every saved reply is shared by the team.

## Use a saved reply

Picking a reply puts its text in the message box with the placeholders filled in. Nothing is sent yet. You can still change the text before you send it. A placeholder without a value stays in the text, so you see what to fill in.

- **In a WhatsApp or SMS message.** On a client or contact, click **Send message** in the Messages section. Choose the channel and pick a reply under **Saved reply**. When the WhatsApp window is closed, you pick an approved template instead.
- **In an answer to the customer.** On a request or complaint ticket, pick a reply above **Message to the customer**. Click **Send answer**. Leave **Also set the ticket to waiting for the customer** ticked to wait for their reaction. The customer reads the answer on the portal.
- **In an email.** On a client or contact, click **Write email** next to an email address. Type a subject, pick a reply and click **Open in Mail**. The Mail app opens the email with everything filled in, and you send it from there. Without the Mail app, your own mail program opens instead.

## Send a failed message again

A WhatsApp or SMS message that failed or expired shows **Send again** in the Messages section. Click it to send the same text, or the same template with the same values. The same checks run as for a new message: consent, budget and the WhatsApp window.

- The new message appears at the top of the list. The failed one keeps its status, so the history stays true.
- When the WhatsApp window has closed, the Send message dialog opens on WhatsApp. Pick an approved template there.
- When the message fails again, the reason shows under the message.

A message that was already sent again shows no button. Failed emails are not sent again from Pipelinq: the Mail app keeps and retries them in its outbox.
